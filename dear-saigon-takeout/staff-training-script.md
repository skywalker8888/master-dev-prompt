# Dear Saigon Takeout — Staff Training Script
## AI Phone Ordering System (Powered by Zest)

**Training time:** ~15 minutes  
**Who needs this:** All front-of-house staff, owner

---

## What the AI Does

Your restaurant now has a **24/7 AI phone agent** at **(647) 932-2932**.

When a customer calls that number:
1. The AI answers immediately (no hold music, no waiting)
2. It greets them: *"Thank you for calling Dear Saigon Takeout!"*
3. It takes their order by item code or item name
4. It sends the order **directly to your Clover POS**
5. It texts the customer a confirmation with their order number and pickup time

**You don't need to do anything to receive these orders** — they appear in Clover exactly like a regular order.

---

## What the AI Does NOT Do

| Scenario | What the AI does |
|---|---|
| Customer wants a reservation | Tells them reservations are by phone only, offers to transfer |
| Customer has a complaint | Offers to transfer to the restaurant |
| Customer asks about allergens | Reads the menu description, advises to call the restaurant directly |
| Customer asks for a discount | Politely declines, no promo codes unless you set them up |
| Unknown question | Says "I'm not sure about that — let me transfer you" |

## How to Spot an AI Order in Clover

AI orders will appear in Clover with:
- **Title:** `TAKEOUT — [customer phone number]`
- **Note:** `Order ID: DS-XXXXXX | Via Zest AI Phone`

When a customer comes to pick up, ask for their **name or last 4 digits of their phone number**.

## Frequently Asked Staff Questions

**Q: What if the AI makes a mistake on the order?**  
A: The customer will see it on their SMS confirmation. If they call back to fix it, take the order manually in Clover and cancel the AI order. You can also check and edit orders in Clover before they go to the kitchen.

**Q: What if we're slammed and can't do the AI's estimated pickup time?**  
A: You can't change the AI's ETA in real time yet. If you need more time, call the customer back at the number shown in Clover.

**Q: Can I turn off the AI?**  
A: Yes. Go to **usezest.ca/settings** → scroll to **Danger Zone** → click **Pause AI**. Do this if you're closing early or need to stop orders. Remember to re-activate it.

**Q: What if someone calls the old number (647) 394-2518?**  
A: That's the human line — answer it normally for reservations and questions. AI orders come in on (647) 932-2932 only.

**Q: Do I need to do anything with the QR codes on the tables?**  
A: No action needed. Customers scan it, browse the menu on their phone, then call the AI number to order. It's just a digital menu.
