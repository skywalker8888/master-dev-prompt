# Dear Saigon Takeout — Branding Guide

## Brand Identity

**Restaurant:** Dear Saigon Takeout  
**Location:** Burlington, ON  
**Cuisine:** Vietnamese — authentic, fresh, elevated  
**Tone:** Warm, welcoming, culturally proud. Not trendy or gimmicky.

## Voice & Tone

**Do:**
- Use Vietnamese dish names first, English in parentheses
- Write descriptions in plain English — no unnecessary adjectives
- Be direct: "Crispy fried rolls" not "Deliciously golden, hand-rolled spring rolls"
- Reference cultural context briefly: "Central Vietnam style", "Hanoi-style"

**Don't:**
- Use trendy food language ("elevated", "artisanal", "curated")
- Emoji in formal menu copy (OK for SMS and social)
- Overpromise on delivery speed — Zest AI gives a calculated ETA

---

## Logo Usage

- Text-only wordmark: **Dear Saigon** (Georgia, no bold)
- Eyebrow above it: **BURLINGTON · VIETNAMESE** (sans-serif, uppercase, gold)
- No icon/symbol required — the name is the logo

---

## Print Menu

- Paper: matte white, A4 or Letter
- Red headers, gold section dividers
- Item codes printed in light grey — used by AI phone agent for unambiguous ordering
- QR code at bottom: links to `TakeOut AI System.dc.html`
